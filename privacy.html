Privacy Policy for Social Battery

Last updated: August 2026

Social Battery ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Phone number: used to create your account and verify your identity via SMS.
  • Contacts: with your permission, we access your device contacts to help you invite friends who use the app, or to invite friends who don't yet use the app via text message. We do not upload or store your full contact list — we only look up phone numbers you choose to invite.
  • Device push token: used to send you notifications about check-ins and plan outcomes.

How Your Votes Are Handled

When you vote on whether you're available for an event, your response is stored without any identifying information attached. We cannot, and do not, know how any individual person voted — only the aggregate outcome of a group's votes.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above solely to operate the app's core features: authentication, event invitations, and push notifications. We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for authentication, data storage, and push notification delivery. Firebase's own privacy practices apply to data processed through their infrastructure: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Your Choices

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Contact us at support@socialbatteryapp.com.
